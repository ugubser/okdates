Say when you're free.
We'll find the day.

No calendars to click through. Everyone writes their availability in a sentence, OkDates reads it and shows the dates that work for the whole group.

Free, no account, nothing to install.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create an event or a meeting

    You get a link to share and a private organizer link. Add a password if you want to edit later from another device.

  2. 2

    Send the link

    Everyone answers on their own, or you type their availability in for them.

  3. 3

    People write when they're free

    In their own words. Once a few have answered, the rest can just tick the dates in the grid.

  4. 4

    Pick the day that works

    The grid highlights the date everyone can make. As organizer you download the calendar invite and send it round.
